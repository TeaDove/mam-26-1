## Conditions for recrystallization

Taking static recrystallization after hot deformation as a prerequisite condition, the effects of various factors on recrystallized grain size have been described so far. It must be stressed, however, that static recrystallization accompanying hot deformation does not occur under all conditions.

On the contrary, it is necessary to apply more deformation than that required for recrystallization. The critical amount of deformation, $R_{crit}$ , required for the completion of recrystallization is small, as is the temperature dependence in plain C steel. In Nb steel, however, $R_{crit}$ is very large and its temperature dependence substantially higher. The effects of deformation temperature and initial grain size on $R_{crit}$ are shown in Fig. 9. In Nb steel, the effect of grain size on $R_{crit}$ is extremely large, and $R_{crit}$ increases rapidly with decreasing deformation temperature. Recrystallization is virtually suppressed below 950 °C.
