It has already been stated that deformed α (ferrite) does not recrystallize, but only recovers because of strain-induced precipitation of Nb(C, N) in the α substructure, which leads to a fine α-grain structure. The sizable influence on grain refinement of deformation in the two-phase region was observed in Nb-free (niobium free) steel too. Priestner and de los Rios reported that when transformation began during rolling, the much greater refinement of the α-grain size resulted from extra intragranular formation of α-grains, indicating that it was the substructure, and not microalloyed precipitates, that caused the extra nucleation of α . Melloy and Dennison and other investigators attributed grain refinement to the dynamic recovery and/or dynamic recrystallization of α in Nb-free steel.
