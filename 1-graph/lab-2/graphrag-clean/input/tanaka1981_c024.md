## Elimination of separation

Various methods have been proposed to prevent the occurrence of separations. In order to eliminate flattened MnS which triggers the initiation of separation, inclusion-shape control has been employed, which converts elongated sulphide to a globular inclusion by the addition of rare earth metals and/or Ca. Advancing the sulphide-shape control technique, a new desulphurization method has been developed, which can reduce the S content to an extremely low level. Finish rolling in the γ-region (austenite region) results in the formation of a weak $\{100\}$ texture, suppressing markedly the occurrence of separation. A decrease in the P content is desirable, since it causes temper embrittlement during slow cooling after coiling in hot-rolled strip. Once initiated separation has a propensity to propagate along the banded structure. Mori et al. reported that the occurrence of separation is determined by the combined effect of band density and $\{100\}$ intensity, and that the addition of Cr disperses a banded structure, thereby suppressing the occurrence of separations.
