\section*{Research Project Passport}

\textbf{Topic.} Comparison of graph neural networks for missing-link prediction in scholarly citation graphs.

\textbf{Motivation.} Missing citation links limit the discovery of related research.
  A link prediction model could suggest candidate references for manual review.

\textbf{Object of study.} A directed citation graph whose vertices are scholarly articles and
  whose edges are citations.

\textbf{Subject of study.} The effect of neighborhood aggregation on missing-link prediction quality.

\textbf{Objective.} Determine whether GraphSAGE improves citation ranking over a degree-based
  heuristic and a matrix factorization baseline.

\textbf{Tasks.} Build a synthetic citation graph, prepare a chronological split, train the models,
  and compare their accuracy and runtime.

\textbf{Method.} Train on citations observed before a cutoff and rank later citations
  among candidate articles available at prediction time. Measure mean reciprocal rank and Hits@10.

\textbf{Expected result.} A reproducible evaluation protocol, a prototype, and a comparison of the methods.

\textbf{Practical application.} Prioritize candidate links in scholarly search and discovery tools.

\textbf{Status.} Illustrative research proposal using synthetic data.
